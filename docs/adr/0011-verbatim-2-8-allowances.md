# 0011. Reserved-term allowances that are 2.8's own texts

- **Status:** Accepted: default, reversible
- **Date:** 2026-09-25

## Context

- **Guardrails 2.8, "Reserved terms"** (`docs/guardrails.md` v1.5): the reserved terms are allowed in action labels, such as "Confirm"; in badges, status lines and generated sentences that the app builds from stored state, such as "Confirmed by you", "Verified by SOVITECH", "designed to provide the functions of BAC class B, verified by SOVITECH" and "Formal quotation" at stage 3; in verbatim document text shown as a quoted excerpt; and in registry qualifier labels, such as "final energy". They are flagged everywhere else. 2.8's badge table and status-line table list the badges and lines the owner sees ("These are the only badges the owner sees on values"; "They are also the only ones used").
- **Guardrails section 10**: "When unsure, treat the change as loosening"; a loosening needs the approver's own words.
- **Prompt 3 section 13**: adding a reserved-term exception is a loosening to stop and ask about; section 5.3: "A badge or status line that is not in guardrails 2.8" is never the build's to decide.
- **The mechanism** (ADR 0005, decision 6; ADR 0008, decision 4): every reserved-term allowance is an entry of `REGISTERED_ALLOWANCE_ENTRIES` in `packages/registry/src/reserved-terms.ts`, a typed entry of one of five kinds (`action_label`, `badge`, `status_line`, `generated_sentence`, `qualifier_label`). The list is an allow list of the loosening check's exception-list snapshot (`reserved-terms.allowances`), so an entry added after the unapproved baseline fails the loosening check until an approved exception-list snapshot holds it.
- **The question** (build log, phase 0, "Questions for the owner", item 3): phase 1 registers the badge and status-line registries, which hold 2.8's texts, and with them the allowances for the texts that carry a reserved term. Does registering, word for word, a text 2.8 names count as "adding a reserved-term exception", which needs the approver? If it does, the loosening check stays red from phase 1 until D-05 (naming the approver) is decided, and phase 1's exit item "the loosening snapshot is green" cannot hold.
- **The ruling** (the orchestrator, 2026-09-25, under prompt 3 section 4): an allowance whose text is word for word a text that guardrails 2.8 lists (a badge label, a status line or its template with placeholders, an action label 2.8 names, a generated sentence 2.8 gives as an example form), and whose kind matches the place 2.8 allows it, is 2.8 itself, not an exception beyond it. The exception-list part of the loosening check accepts such an allowance when it can match it verbatim against the tables of `docs/guardrails.md` 2.8 at run time; every other allowance still waits for the approver.
- **The reading this ADR takes of "the tables of 2.8"** (stated in the phase 1 review, verifier finding 12, for the owner): the ruling's words say "match it verbatim against the tables of 2.8", but its own list of what counts names "an action label 2.8 names" and "a generated sentence 2.8 gives as an example form", and 2.8's tables hold no action label and not every example sentence. So this ADR reads "2.8's texts" as the badge table, the status-line table **and** 2.8's "Where they are allowed" list: the quoted examples of its action-label bullet ("Confirm") and of its bullet on badges, status lines and generated sentences ("designed to provide the functions of BAC class B, verified by SOVITECH", and "Formal quotation" with its condition "at stage 3"). This is wider than "the tables" alone. It has no effect on what is accepted today: all five accepted allowances are texts of the tables. The narrower reading (tables only) is listed under "How to reverse"; the owner may choose it.
- **D rows.** D-05 (the approver; open). The owner decided no D row as of 2026-09-25.

## Decision

1. **The loosening check reads 2.8 at run time** (`tools/checks/loosening/guardrails-2-8.ts`) and gives each text the allowance kind of the place it comes from:
   - the badge table, "Badge" column (each bold label): `badge`;
   - the badge table, "Example line" column (each quoted sentence): `generated_sentence`, the line the app builds under the badge;
   - the status-line table, "Text" column (each quoted text): `status_line`;
   - the "Where they are allowed" list: the quoted examples of the action-label bullet are `action_label`; the quoted examples of the bullet on badges, status lines and generated sentences that neither table lists are `generated_sentence` (today the BAC-class sentence of rule 11). An example both lists name keeps the table's kind only, so "Verified by SOVITECH" is a badge, never a generated sentence.
2. **Word for word.** After Unicode NFC and single spaces, the allowance's text equals the 2.8 text character for character; case, punctuation and every word count. Two readings of a slot only:
   - a placeholder 2.8 writes as `<name>` ("Superseded: inputs changed on <date>") may be a `{slot}` of any name;
   - in a status line or a generated sentence, an example value 2.8 writes as digits ("126", "37 of 40") or as a day and month ("12 Oct") may be a `{slot}`: the ruling's "its template with placeholders" and "example form". A word of 2.8's example stays a word: "BAC class {class}" is not "BAC class B", and waits for the approver.
3. **The kind must match.** "Verified by SOVITECH" as a `status_line`, or "Formal quotation" as a `badge`, waits for the approver.
4. **Only 2.8 as main holds it.** 2.8 is read at the merge base of `main` and HEAD (where approvals are read from, ADR 0005 decision 3) and in the working tree, and a text counts only when both list it for the same kind. The build branch cannot add a badge to its own 2.8 and register an allowance for it; a text 2.8 no longer lists is not accepted either. When git cannot give the merge base, nothing is accepted, and the check says why.
5. **What the check does with such an allowance.** In the exception-list part of the loosening check, an allowance entry added to `reserved-terms.allowances`, or changed, that matches is accepted without an approval reference and reported on its own line ("a text of 2.8 (ADR 0011)"). It is never recorded in the snapshot: the baseline writer leaves it out and says so, so each run checks it against 2.8 again, and an approved change of 2.8 on main that removes the text makes the check fail on the allowance. Every other added or changed allowance is a loosening that waits for the approver, as before.
6. **Outside the ruling, and so waiting for the approver:** registry qualifier labels (2.8 names "final energy" as an example, but the ruling names badge labels, status lines, action labels and generated sentences only); anything close to a 2.8 text but not the same; a 2.8 text with another kind; a slot where 2.8 has a word.
7. **Slots carry no reserved term.** The allowance matcher (`createAllowanceSet` in `packages/registry/src/reserved-terms.ts`) covers a copy unit with a generated sentence's template only when no text filling a slot holds a reserved term itself, so "AI inference, verified by SOVITECH on {date}" does not carry "firm price" through its date slot. This tightens the matcher; it is needed once a 2.8 template with a slot can pass without the approver.
8. **The owner still sees each one.** Accepted allowances are entries of an allow list, so they are listed for the owner in `docs/build-log.md`, "For the owner's review" (the loosening check fails when one is missing there), and in the check's details with the words "accepted as 2.8 itself under ADR 0011, which the owner may reverse".
9. **Seeded inputs** (the loosening self-test, `tools/checks/loosening/seeded/`): the control `good-2-8-badge-allowance/` (the badge "Verified by SOVITECH" and the sentence "AI inference, verified by SOVITECH on {date}") must pass; `exception-list-allowance-near-miss-2-8/` ("Verified by SOVITECH engineers"), `exception-list-allowance-2-8-wrong-kind/` ("Verified by SOVITECH" as a status line), `exception-list-allowance-2-8-only-in-working-tree/` (a badge the working tree alone adds to 2.8) and `exception-list-allowance-qualifier-label/` ("final energy") must each fail, waiting for the approver. Unit tests: `tools/checks/loosening/exception-lists.test.ts` (the parser, the texts, eleven refusals, the evaluation) and `packages/registry/src/reserved-terms.test.ts` (a slot that carries a reserved term).

## Consequences

- **Truth: unchanged.** The texts accepted are the ones 2.8 allows, in the places 2.8 allows them; the matcher still flags the same text anywhere else, and a slot cannot carry a reserved term through. "Formal quotation" as a `status_line` passes the copy check wherever it is marked as a status line; that it appears only at stage 3 is the Price component's job (rule 10, phase 5; F-REGISTRY-05), not the allowance's.
- **Speed: up.** Phase 1 can register the badge and status-line registries with the allowances for 2.8's own texts, and the loosening check stays green on them while no approver is named (D-05).
- **What waits.** A qualifier label ("final energy", which rule 8's energy qualifier will want), a template with a slot where 2.8 has a word (the BAC class letter, phase 5 or 7), and any wording beyond 2.8. Each is listed for the approver when a phase registers it.
- **The owner's review.** This ADR, and each allowance it accepts, are listed for the owner to confirm or reverse (build log, "Questions for the owner" and "For the owner's review").

## How to reverse

- **The whole ruling:** remove the 2.8 acceptance from `evaluateExceptionLists` and `planExceptionListBaseline` in `tools/checks/loosening/exception-lists.ts` (the `ALLOWANCES_LIST` branch and `allowanceAs2_8`), with `guardrails-2-8.ts`, the control seed `good-2-8-badge-allowance/` and the four seeds above. Every allowance then waits for the approver again, and the loosening check stays red on each until D-05 is decided. This is a tightening; it needs no approver.
- **Narrower readings** (no example-value slots; tables only, not the "Where they are allowed" list, which is the reading of "the tables of 2.8" the Context states): change `patternOf` or `parse2_8` in `guardrails-2-8.ts` with their tests. Each accepts less, so each is a tightening.
- **Wider readings** (qualifier labels, word slots, reading 2.8 from the working tree alone): each accepts more text without the approver, so each is a loosening under guardrails section 10 and needs the approver's own words.
- **The slot rule** of decision 7: removing it lets a template carry a reserved term through a slot, a loosening.

## Phase 1 review amendment (2026-09-25)

The verifier's finding 12 and the adversarial reviewer's finding 12 (probe-terms, confirmed): the source reserved-term check applied all five accepted allowances to every copy unit, whatever its place, so "Verified by SOVITECH", "Confirmed by you" and "Formal quotation" passed in any string (a stage 2 export heading, a demo seed, a string describing an unconfirmed value); the `{date}` slot took any words, so "AI inference, verified by SOVITECH on request of the designer" (rule 14's own example of a document claim) passed as the generated sentence; and "Formal quotation" held with no stage 3 condition. 2.8 allows these texts only as "badges, status lines and generated sentences that the app builds from stored state", flags them "everywhere else", and allows "Formal quotation" "at stage 3" only; rule 10 derives stage 3 from a stored quotation record. Each change below accepts less text than before, so each is a tightening; none adds a question, gate, confirmation or wording.

10. **Allowances hold only in the copy registries, in source.** The source scan (`tools/checks/reserved-terms/scan.ts`, `allowanceScope`, `COPY_REGISTRIES` = `packages/registry/src/copy/**`) matches copy units inside the copy registries with the registered allowances, as their definitions (`scanCopy` context `copy_registry`), and every other copy unit with none. The same text anywhere else in source is flagged, with the note "a registered allowance's text outside the copy registries: take it from the registry". A copy-registry glob that matches no scanned file fails while allowances are registered.
11. **Typed slots.** A template's slots (generated sentences and status lines) take a closed list of names, each with a type (`SLOT_TYPES` in `packages/registry/src/reserved-terms.ts`): `{date}` takes a calendar date as the app writes one ("12 Oct", "25 Sep 2026", "2026-09-25"; "31 Feb" and "29 Feb 2027" are refused), `{count}`, `{analysed}` and `{total}` take digits with their separators. A word never fills a slot. A slot holds its own placeholder (`{date}`) only as the copy registry's definition of the text, never an interpolation read as `{}`. A slot name with no type fails `createAllowanceSet`. Adding a slot name or a type accepts more text, so it is listed for the approver with the phase that needs it.
12. **The stage 3 label is bound to its stored record.** An allowance of kind `status_line` may carry `requiresRecord: 'quotation_record'`; it then holds only when the copy is derived from a named stored quotation record (`scanCopy`'s `quotationRecordId`), or as its registry definition. The registered "Formal quotation" carries it (`packages/registry/src/copy/status-lines.ts`, `readsRecord`; `allowances.ts`). The loosening check's 2.8 reading marks a text the "Where they are allowed" list allows only "at stage 3" as bound to that record (`guardrails-2-8.ts`), and accepts the allowance as 2.8's text only with the same binding: unbound, it waits for the approver.
13. **Rendered pages.** The render check (`tests/e2e/render/rendered-copy.ts`) honours a `badge`, `status-line` or `generated-sentence` marker only when a display object the screen was served carries that text among its `lines` (2.8: "builds from stored state"), with its slots typed as above, and the stage 3 label only when that display object names its stored quotation record (`ServedDisplay.quotationRecordId`, new in `tests/e2e/render/contract.ts`).
14. **The AI output validator** (phase 2) scans AI-written text with `NO_ALLOWANCES`, never with the registered set: 2.8 flags reserved terms in "all AI-written text", and the app, not the AI, builds every badge, status line and generated sentence (documented on `NO_ALLOWANCES` and `REGISTERED_ALLOWANCE_ENTRIES`).
15. **Seeded inputs and tests.** Reserved-term self-test: `bad-allowance-outside-copy-registry/` (the five texts in a screen, an export template, a seed and a data file, with the registered allowances; the copy registry of the same tree stays unflagged), `bad-allowance-slot-not-typed/` (words, an impossible day and an interpolation in `{date}`, a word in `{count}`; controls unflagged) and `bad-allowance-untyped-slot/` (a slot with no type); the good tree now writes its allowed texts in its own copy registry. Loosening self-test: `exception-list-allowance-2-8-stage-3-unbound/` ("Formal quotation" without its record binding waits for the approver). Unit tests: `packages/registry/src/reserved-terms.test.ts` (typed slots, number slots, registry definitions, untyped slots, the stage 3 binding), `packages/registry/src/copy/copy.test.ts` (the registry texts as definitions and as shown; the binding), `tools/checks/loosening/exception-lists.test.ts` (the unbound and wrongly bound stage 3 label refused; the binding read from 2.8), `tools/checks/render/rendered-copy.test.ts` (served lines, typed slots and the stage 3 record on rendered pages).

**Consequences of the amendment.** Truth: up; the five texts pass only where 2.8 allows them. Speed: none for the owner (no question, gate or confirmation). For the build: a screen shows a badge, a status line or a generated sentence by taking it from the copy registries through the view-model's display objects, never by writing the text again; phase 5's Price component must pass the stored quotation record id for the stage 3 label to pass the render check.

**How to reverse the amendment:** each part (the copy-registry scope, the slot types, the record binding, the served-lines rule on rendered pages) is removed where it is named above, with its seeds and tests. Each removal lets more text through, so each is a loosening that needs the approver's own words.
