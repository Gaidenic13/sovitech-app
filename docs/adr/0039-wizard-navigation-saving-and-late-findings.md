# 0039. Wizard navigation, saving, late findings and Generate before phase 5

- **Status:** Accepted: default, reversible
- **Date:** 2026-09-30

## Context

Prompt 3 5.2 gives defaults for the wizard's open questions; prompt 3 section 4 says that where a 5.2 default and the PRD's "Until decided" line differ, the stricter one is followed and logged. The lines, quoted:

- **5.2 "Wizard saving and navigation"** (onboarding Q9, Q10): "Every answer the owner makes is stored when made (it is an event anyway). Accepting a visible suggestion (rule 3) and skipping an unanswered question (rule 7) happen only on Continue, never on save. Resume at the last visited step. The stepper opens any visited step. After an Edit from step 8, Continue returns to step 8."
- **PRD R-008 "Until decided"** (D-10): "clicking the stepper opens nothing; Back, Continue and step 8's Edit links move the owner, and Continue after an Edit moves to the next step in order."
- **PRD R-009 "Until decided"** (D-10): "answers saved by Next, Continue, an inline Edit or an inline ask stay stored as candidates and events, and unsaved input is not stored; a project reopened before Generate opens at step 1 with every stored answer shown and nothing asked again, and a project with a generated proposal opens its stored proposal, not the wizard."
- **5.2 "Generate before phase 5"** (onboarding Q12): "Generate goes to the generating state, then Overview, whose estimate reads 'Not available yet' naming what is missing. The button is never dead." **PRD 10.4, UD-01:** "Until dashboards 8.3 and onboarding Q12 are answered, no separate Overview is built and the owner sees the stored proposal (US-PROPOSAL-05)."
- **Rule 7, "Late findings never interrupt":** no dialog, no move, no changed answer; a dot on the step and the review list; one quiet notice. G7-4.
- **5.2 "No documents"** (onboarding Q1) and **PRD R-047 "Until decided"** (D-21): no manual-entry form; with no document, each fact reads Not provided yet with Edit.
- **5.2 "No IFC uploaded"** (onboarding Q3): the view area shows "Not available yet", naming the missing model, with the upload action. **US-REVIEW-04 AC14:** no viewer, view toggle or floor selector while onboarding Q3 is open.
- **5.2 "Unchecking a system after step 7"** (onboarding Q4) and **PRD R-005 "Until decided"** (D-12): step 7 preselects nothing; unticking a step 4 system changes no automation-area answer.
- **5.2 "Project type branching"** (onboarding Q5) and **PRD R-010 "Until decided"** (D-23): one single choice; the same questions for every type.
- **5.2 "City"**: "Country first from ISO 3166-1. The city is stored as the owner typed it, and the city id stays Unknown until the SIRUTA licence is confirmed."

## Decision

1. **Saving follows R-009 (stricter than 5.2):** an answer is stored by Next or Continue, an inline Edit save, a confirmation, a step 8 inline ask or "Skip for now"; unsaved input is not stored. A visible suggestion is accepted only on Continue (rule 3). The contract's writes are `steps.continue` and the `fields.*` routes (ADR 0036).
2. **Navigation follows R-008 and R-009 (stricter than 5.2):** no resume state is stored; a project opens at step 1 with every stored answer shown and nothing asked again; the stepper is not clickable; Back and Continue move one step; step 8's Edit links open their step, and Continue then moves to the next step in order. A project with a stored proposal would open it (none exists before phase 5).
3. **Late findings** keep a ledger in the page session only: for each step the owner has left, the `asOf` time of the step view they left. The web polls `GET /api/projects/:projectId/late-findings` every 10 s with the ledger; the server derives from stored state the steps with a finding after the owner left them (dots) and the count of findings since the previous poll (the one quiet notice, a bound line). A finding is a new eligible candidate that is not the owner's own, or a conflict whose newest candidate arrived after, on a field of that step (the registry question's step; step 3 for building facts). No dialog, no move, no changed answer; the notice is a polite live region the owner may dismiss; the findings join step 8 through the field states. A step left in this page session renders as completed (check and title, no number), so its dot sits in an item the render test does not read as a step number.
4. **Generate (5.2 read with PRD 10.4):** never disabled; phase 3 stores nothing (no proposal snapshot exists before the phase 5 engine, and none is invented); it shows the generating state (UD-07: only "Still reading <n> files…", no figure) while `GET /api/projects/:projectId/proposal` loads, then phase 3's proposal page: each output's "Not available yet" line naming what is missing (the SOVITECH datasets behind the closed gates; an owner input with its Add action to step 8's inline ask), and a link back to step 8. No separate Overview (UD-01).
5. **No documents:** no manual-entry form; step 3's facts read Not provided yet with Edit (R-047); Continue opens step 4; missing first-estimate fields are asked once, inline, at step 8.
6. **Step 3's view area:** no viewer, toggle or floor selector (US-REVIEW-04 AC14). With no IFC model stored: "Not available yet" naming the missing model, with the action to add one on step 2. With a model stored (the demo's two): its 2.8 line "Not analysed: IFC model stored, not analysed". Never an illustrative or mockup building.
7. **Step 7:** no preselection; a later step 4 change leaves every automation answer as it is; the departure shows as information (rule 4, decisions).
8. **Project type:** one single choice; steps 2 to 8 are the same for every type.
9. **Location:** the country first, from the ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 codes (names from the browser's `Intl.DisplayNames`); the city typed as text; no city list and no city id (D-94); changing the country clears the city.

10. **Amended 2026-10-01 (the integrator, recording the web builders' readings; each default, reversible):**
   - **Step 3** (the steps 3-4 builder): the summary rows are read-only in their value element, with Edit beside them; every other owner response (the confirmation, "Looks right", "Something's wrong", a conflict's "Choose this value") sits once, in "Extracted details", which the pill counts. The same value id shows the same text, badge, source and lines in both places (G2-7). A conflict put to the owner offers "Choose this value" on step 3 as well as on step 8 (new Q17, D-14, open; one line in `apps/web/src/steps/step-3/FactRows.tsx` to take it off step 3).
     *Amended in part B, 2026-10-01 (DR-3):* step 3's summary rows carry no actions at all, Edit included: they are onboarding-spec 2.5's read-only key/value list. Edit, the confirmation, "Looks right", "Something's wrong" and a conflict's "Choose this value" sit only in "Extracted details", once each (the duplicated Edit of part A is gone). G2-7 still holds: the summary and the details show the same value id with the identical text, badge, source and lines.
   - **Reading in place:** steps 3 and 4 read their view again every 5 s while anything reads "Reading documents…", step 2 every 3 s while a file is in progress, and step 8 at each late-findings notice and every poll while "Still reading N files" shows; rows fill in place, with no dialog.
   - **UD-45** keeps the stepper at step 3, and opening it records step 3 as left in the late-findings ledger; "Back to your building" opens step 3 and stores nothing.
   - **Step 4** never ticks, or reports as a suggestion, a life-safety or never-preselected card from served data (Fire Safety, Access Control, Elevators); a card the owner touches hides its decision display until Continue. Continue sends the whole question with the ticked decision fields; with nothing ticked the server records a skip, never an exclusion of every system.
   - **Step 8's Generate** first skips, once each, the inline asks left unanswered (rule 7: pressing Continue on an unanswered question skips it; "Skip means skip"), then sends step 8's Continue and opens the proposal page; "Generate without it" is the same press. A skip the server refuses never holds Generate back.
   - **The proposal page's "Add <field>"** opens step 8 with the navigation state `{ add: fieldKey }`, which focuses that field's inline ask, or its card's Edit link when no ask is left (R-012 "Until decided": never an empty page).
   - **The systems inline ask on step 8** saves one `fields/edit` per system (include for ticked, exclude for unticked), taking the option keys from each field's served Edit action, and asks for at least one tick or "Generate without it" (R-051 interim reading). No multi-write route exists; a failure part-way leaves what was written, and the step reads its view again, so what is stored is what shows.
   - **Uploads** run at project level: at most two at a time, still running while the owner moves on to step 3 (US-DOCS-03 AC9), stopped by "Stop uploading", lost when the page reloads (the server sweeps an abandoned upload after 30 minutes, ADR 0019). *Amended in part B (DR-10):* a file the API refuses after creating its upload shows the name its refusal serves (`upload:<id>.fileName`, through Value); a file refused on the page shows its extension in words when it is one to four letters ("A EXE file was not added"), else "A file was not added".
   - **The demo line while loading or failing** (part B, V-1): what any response of the page session said about a project's demo flag (the list row, a screen envelope, the list's Open in router state) is kept by project id in the session (`rememberProject`), so the demo's screens show the served line while their requests are pending or after they failed (G10-12). Residual: a cold load of a demo screen in a new page session whose every request fails cannot know the flag, and shows no line until one answers.
   - **Skips name their page** (part B, G7-10; ADR 0036 decision 11): every "Skip for now", step 8's Generate and "Generate without it" send the step of the page they were pressed on (4, 5, 6, 7 or 8), so the API records a skip only where its question is asked now; the contract requires it.
   - **"Add <field>" after Generate** (part B, V-4a; G7-11): step 8 opened with the navigation state `{ add: fieldKey }` reads its view with `?add=<fieldKey>`, so the API serves that field's inline ask again even after Generate skipped it (the owner asked for it: PRD R-012 "Until decided"), and the page focuses it; the card's Edit link stays the fallback.
   - **Uploads completing together** in a new project add the extraction service account as a member one at a time (`lockProjectMembership`, a transaction-level advisory lock keyed by the project, in `packages/db/src/guarded.ts`); before, the second of two parallel completions failed on the members' key (found by the e2e flow with fixture uploads; `tests/api/uploads-concurrent-completion.test.ts`).

11. **Amended 2026-10-01 (phase 3, the fix round after part B's final verification; the web; each default, reversible):**
   - **One request per press.** Every owner write sends one request per press: `useInFlight` (`apps/web/src/wizard/use-in-flight.ts`) holds the guard in a ref, so a second press is ignored even when it arrives before React renders again (a double click, Enter then a click). The control is never disabled (rule 7): it shows `aria-busy` while its request is on its way and takes presses again once the answer is in; after a refusal the hook releases it, and after a success that moves the owner on, the control leaves with its page. Wired on the new project's Next and its form's Enter submit, step 1's Next, Continue on steps 2 to 7, step 4's "Skip for now", step 5's skip and "Yes, it's a hotel", the skips of steps 6 and 7, Generate and "Generate without it" (one guard), each inline ask's Save, and InlineEditor's Save; the field writes of a screen (skip, Yes, "Looks right", "Something's wrong", "Choose this value") run one at a time. The skips, step 5's and step 8's For you writes and the inline ask's Save stay busy until the view is read again, so a second press never acts on what the first one changed. The kit's `SkipForNow` takes an optional `busy`. The server makes a second request a no-op as well (ADR 0036 decision 13); the guard does not cover two tabs or two clients, which the server's lock does.
   - **Step 8's "changed" message.** When an inline ask's Save is refused `shown_value_changed` and the view, read again, no longer serves that ask, step 8 shows the catalogue's existing `edit.changed` sentence once, beside the alert icon, where the asks are, until the owner leaves the step or the ask is served again (no new wording).
   - **The Proposal card's stage** (rules 7 and 10; G10-11 as corrected): the card names the served stage label only while the investment output carrying that stage is served as a range, or `proposal.stage` is non-null; otherwise it shows that output's served "Not available yet: …" line, bound, with its "Add <field>" action, which focuses the inline ask; with no stage served, it names none. In this build (every dataset gate closed) it shows the line of "Preliminary investment estimate"'s output, naming the SOVITECH datasets and any missing first-estimate input.
   - **Not yet:** the kit's Value action buttons (Yes, "Looks right", "Something's wrong", Edit, Add) show no `aria-busy` while a write is on its way (the caller's guard ignores the second press); step 3's FactRows guard is per row and released at the answer, so a press on another row while one write is on its way still sends (a double press on one control sends one request).

## Consequences

- An owner who reloads the page mid-intake starts again at step 1 with every stored answer shown; dots are relative to the page session. Resume at the last visited step waits for D-10.
- The stepper's completed state is a page-session state, not a stored one: a step opened directly (a reload or a link) shows no completed step before it (part B, DR-21; the build log's "Waiting" table, D-10). Once D-10 is decided, the done state derives from the served step state.
- Phase 5 replaces the proposal page with the stored proposal and records the snapshot on Generate.

## How to reverse

When the owner decides D-10: add a per-user intake-progress record (last visited step, left times) in a new migration, make the stepper open visited steps and resume there; the late-findings route already takes the left times from its caller. When D-14 is decided: point Generate at the chosen landing.
