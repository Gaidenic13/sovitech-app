# 0040. The app theme tokens and the UI kit's styling: the brand's dark variant, Brand values only

- **Status:** Accepted: default, reversible (the Brand rows are the owner's decision of 2026-09-24; which proposed rows enter the app is this build's reading, listed for the owner)
- **Date:** 2026-09-30 (planner); amended 2026-09-30 by the UI kit builder (phase 3, part A); amended 2026-10-01 by the kit fixer (phase 3, part B: the design review's kit findings, decision 6); amended 2026-10-02 by the kit fixer (phase 4, part B: the design review's kit findings, decision 7's "Phase 4, part B" list)

## Context

- **The owner's decision of 2026-09-24** (OD-1 to OD-4; prompt 3 5.1): the app is a SOVITECH brand tool in the brand's dark variant: the real logo (`logo-white.svg`, h-8), surfaces `#07201C` and `#0D2E2B`, green `#1F6B4A`, mint `#C8E6C9` as the single accent on dark, Inter, radii 1px on controls and 2px on surfaces, no shadows. The mockups' teal-navy and aqua theme, wordmark and taglines are replaced.
- **`company/brand/app-alignment.md`, "App theme"** proposes a token per role, each with a Source: "Brand" (decided by the owner's decision), "Brand value, new role" (a brand token in a role the brand does not define on dark; needs the owner's OK) and "Extension" (a value the brand does not have; needs the owner's OK). App-alignment decision 7 and dashboards 8.2 are open (PRD D-19).
- **PRD R-001 Sources and R-138:** "only rows whose source is 'Brand' are adopted; 'Brand value, new role' and 'Extension' rows, such as the upcoming-step ring, disabled and focus ring, stay proposed, D-19". **R-147 "Until decided":** every colour the brand does not define comes from the token file, marked as awaiting the owner's OK. **Prompt 3 5.2, "Extension tokens and title role":** the proposed values in one tokens file marked "proposed, pending the owner's OK".
- **The orchestrator's working rule for phase 3 (stricter):** extension colours marked in "App theme" as proposals are not approved: do not use them; list them. The UI kit builder reads "Brand value, new role" rows as proposals too (they "need the owner's OK", and R-001 says they stay proposed), so no colour of either kind is used.
- **Guardrails 2.8 "Prominence"** (badges 12px or larger, AA contrast, never only on hover) and **WCAG 2.2 AA** (prompt 3 5.2; 1.4.3 text contrast 4.5:1, 1.4.11 non-text contrast 3:1 for control boundaries and focus indicators, 2.4.7 and 2.4.11 focus visible) win over any colour choice.
- **Lint bans** (ADR 0007): colour literals only in `packages/ui/src/tokens.css`; no `box-shadow`, Tailwind `shadow-*` or `ring-*`; focus rings with `outline`. **Render contract** (ADR 0006): icons at 32px or less; no number in generated content or list markers.

## Decision

1. **`packages/ui/src/tokens.css` holds Brand values only**, copied by hand from "App theme" (a comment names the file; nothing reads `company/`). Each token names its row.
   - **Brand (decided):** `--sov-bg` `#07201C`; `--sov-surface` `#0D2E2B`; `--sov-green` `#1F6B4A`; `--sov-accent` mint `#C8E6C9` (the accent row: the current step, checked controls, the active segment, selected borders, the focus ring, text selection and inline links); `--sov-on-accent` `#0D2E2B`; `--sov-surface-selected` mint 8%; `--sov-text-primary` white; `--sov-text-tertiary` white 60%; `--sov-text-muted` white 55% ("Brand range"); `--sov-border` white 10% and `--sov-border-hover` white 25%; the primary button white with a `#0D2E2B` label, mint on hover; the secondary button's white 15% border and white 50% label; text selection mint on `#0D2E2B`; radii 1px, 2px and 9999px (badges, dots, radios only); Inter at 300, 400, 500 and 600; colour-only motion at 150ms and 300ms.
   - **Not used (listed for the owner in the build log, phase 3 "Waiting"):** every "Extension" row (`border-input` and `border-dashed` white 35%, the upcoming-step ring, `control-off`, disabled, `text-secondary` white 80%, the success banner, `status-warning` and the status pill, the other-badges neutral style, the four status colours, the `sys-*` palette, the chart ramp's middle steps) and every "Brand value, new role" colour (mint 50% for completed steps, the sand and lavender badge families). The planner's first version of this ADR used the latter under R-147's interim; the kit builder took them out under the orchestrator's rule and R-001's Sources.
2. **Roles a screen still needs take the nearest Brand value; the ROLE is marked "proposed, pending the owner's OK" in tokens.css:**
   - control boundaries (inputs, the select, the dropzone's dashed line, checkbox and radio rings, the upcoming step's ring): `--sov-control-border`, white 55% (5.94:1 on bg), so they keep 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11);
   - the focus ring: mint (the accent row lists it), `outline` 2px with a 2px offset (prompt 3 section 11);
   - badges: one neutral pill for all twenty 2.8 badges, white label (17:1) and a white 25% hairline; the words carry the meaning, never a colour;
   - completed steps: a white 55% ring with a white check (no mint 50%); the current step alone is mint;
   - the late-findings dot: white, never amber, with a text description;
   - the info banner and the notice: the surface fill with a hairline and a white icon (a raised panel, a Brand role); no success or warning banner (their colours are Extension rows);
   - inline errors: an icon, the words, `aria-invalid` and a brighter (white) border; no error hue exists.
3. **The UI kit's styles** live in `packages/ui/src/ui.css`, plain CSS on `.sov-*` classes, in Tailwind's `components` cascade layer, reading only `var(--sov-*)`. Reasons: the kit's harness pages (tests/e2e/pages/ui/) load the same stylesheet from `file://` with no build step, so the render test and axe check the components exactly as styled; the classes are stable for the component tests; and Tailwind utilities in the app can still place a component. Every kit element uses `box-sizing: border-box`, so it looks the same with or without Tailwind's preflight.
4. **Tailwind wiring** (`apps/web/src/styles.css`): imports `@sovitech/ui/tokens.css` and `@sovitech/ui/ui.css`; lists its sources (never `company/`); switches off the default palette, every shadow scale, the default radii, fonts, font weights, animations and blurs; never generates `ring`, `inset-ring`, `shadow`, `inset-shadow`, `drop-shadow` or `text-shadow` whatever the sources say (`@source not inline`); maps the tokens to theme classes: colours `bg`, `surface`, `surface-selected`, `green`, `accent`, `on-accent`, `text-primary`, `text-tertiary`, `text-muted`, `border`, `border-hover`, `control-border` (so `bg-surface`, `text-text-muted`, `border-border`); radii `rounded-control`, `rounded-surface`, `rounded-pill`; `font-sans` (Inter Variable, self-hosted by `@fontsource-variable/inter`, ADR 0035); weights `font-light`, `font-normal`, `font-medium`, `font-semibold` (no `font-bold`); a base layer with the page colours, a global `:focus-visible` outline, the one page-title role (34px, weight 300, tracking -0.05em: proposed, pending the owner's OK, R-147 interim) and `prefers-reduced-motion`.
5. **Rules the kit enforces in code:** `Button` has no `disabled` state (rule 7: Continue and Generate are never disabled); no value element animates or transitions (G2-8); the stepper's list style is inline (an ordered list draws numbered markers until a stylesheet loads, and the render test reads a marker as an unbound number); every icon is a Lucide icon at 16, 24 or 32px, decorative, stroke 1.5.

6. **Phase 3, part B: the design review's kit findings** (Brand values only; no new colour; each new ROLE marked "proposed, pending the owner's OK" in `tokens.css`):
   - **Two heading roles (DR-18).** Under the one page-title role, `--sov-heading-section-*` (20px, weight 300, 28px leading: a heading inside a step, class `sov-heading-section`) and `--sov-heading-group-*` (16px, weight 500, 24px leading: a group or question label, class `sov-heading-group`; ChoiceGroup's legend and TextField's label read it). Roles proposed, pending the owner's OK; the values are Brand type. Card titles stay 17px at 600.
   - **Tabular figures only on numeric displays (DR-11).** `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` applies to a value's figure parts when the display is numeric (a quantity or count with its unit, a range, a line built around its numbers, a record whose every part is a number alone, such as a document's coverage; `data-numeric="true"` on the value element), to a price, the header date, the stepper's numbers and the character counter. Text values (file names, project names, choices) keep Inter's proportional figures and punctuation.
   - **Served text is isolated (A-8).** The value's text renders in a `<bdi>` with `unicode-bidi: isolate`, so a direction control in owner text never reorders a badge or the copy around it.
   - **A badge stays with its figure (DR-1, DR-2; 2.8 "Prominence").** The value line never wraps between text and badge; a long text wraps inside its own box. The `row` layout is a wrapping flex row: label left, value and badge right on one line where both fit (a 4-column card), the whole value under the label otherwise. A new `compact` layout (the value's text and badge on one line in 14px, any served source or status line below) fills ChoiceCard's new `status` slot on the card's top row beside the icon; in a narrow slot the badge moves under its text, on the same tile (a 4-column card at 1220px leaves about 164px beside the icon and the checkbox, less than a short choice and a 12px badge need side by side; 2.8's 12px minimum is kept). On a tile and a pill, too narrow for a value beside an icon and a control, the slot takes its own row under the title.
   - **The qualifier label (DR-27)** follows the measure after a comma ("Gross floor area, gross total (Scd)"), alone in its `registry-qualifier` element; the kit adds no bracket.
   - **Progress (DR-4).** What is in progress shows as text beside the bar (13px, text-tertiary; fixed copy or a served line with its marker; a hidden mode keeps the accessible name). The track is outlined in `--sov-control-border` (white 55%, 5.94:1; WCAG 1.4.11) and the mint segment is 4px high inside it; under reduced motion the segment stands still.
   - **Links (DR-24).** A link-variant button with no icon is underlined at a 3px offset, as the quiet variant is: mint alone does not tell it from white text (WCAG 1.4.1).
   - **"Skip for now" placement (DR-14).** The link and its "later" line sit 24px under the cards at the grid's left edge, after a grid or in ChoiceGroup's footer (whose 12px gap counts toward it).
   - **Icon sizes (DR-26).** Unchanged and now under test: 32px on tall cards and in the dropzone (the render test's limit, ADR 0035), 24px on tiles, pills, rows and inputs.

7. **Phase 4: the workspace kit** (2026-10-02; Brand values only; no new colour, no new role colour; ADR 0043 for what the frame holds):
   - **Shell sizes** in `tokens.css`, sizes only, from App theme "Shell sizes" (the specs' proposals, dashboards-spec 3.4): `--sov-sidebar-width` 208px, `--sov-sidebar-row` 32px, `--sov-inspector-width` 360px, `--sov-footer-height` 48px, `--sov-gutter` 16px, `--sov-panel-gap` 12px.
   - **One mark for "this one".** The sidebar's current page, the register's open row, the chosen option of a list and the checked chip take the App theme's "Sidebar selected row" (a Brand row): a 2px mint edge (the tab's underline, the chip's border) on the mint 8% fill, with the label at weight 600, so the mark never rests on colour alone. Row hover is the raised surface (`--sov-surface`).
   - **No pill on a control.** The switch is a square-cornered track (the control radius) with a square knob that moves to the end and turns the track mint; the only 9999px radii stay the badge, the radio and the stepper's marks (now under test).
   - **Tables.** The register's header band is the raised surface (App theme: "table header bands"), hairline rows, 14px cells; a value in a cell keeps its text and badge together and, in a narrow column, moves the badge under its text inside the cell (the compact layout's reading of "the same line or tile"), words never split; the badge column (7.1-r2, 7.1.1-E5) holds one value's badge on its row, bound to the same value id (a missing value whose wording is its badge keeps its pill in its own cell: part B below). A table wider than its column scrolls inside a named region that is a tab stop while it scrolls (WCAG 2.1.1; part B below). The same wrapping applies to values in the 208px sidebar (the project card).
   - **The status footer** is a 48px bar on the page colour under a hairline, sticky at the window's foot; the demo line sits in it as a line of the bar (no strip of its own), in full, never truncated.
   - **The model area** (no viewer is wired: the owner's answer of 2026-10-02) is a hairline area with a 32px decorative icon, the heading, the served line and the action: no drawing of a model, plan or building of any kind (R-080).
   - **The eyebrow** (Documents only, ADR 0043 decision 7) follows App theme "Eyebrow" (a Brand row): mint, 14px, weight 600, uppercase, tracked, after a bullet that is a hidden character, never generated content.
   - **Undesigned states** (the inline panels, the menus, the empty register, the model area, the selection list, the active filters) were drawn with the `frontend-design` skill inside the brand; the approved screenshots and the brand win over the skill's warnings (uppercase eyebrow, near-black with one accent).
   - **Phase 4, part B: the design review's kit findings** (2026-10-02; Brand values only, no new colour or role, no shadow, no gradient; radii unchanged):
     - **Radios never take the mixed state (DR-1; case G7-17).** The mint fill and dash of a part selection apply to `.sov-check__input[type='checkbox']:indeterminate` only. HTML calls every radio of a group with no radio checked `:indeterminate`, so the earlier unscoped rule drew every option of an unanswered question as a filled mint disc (step 1's project type, step 5's questions, the revision panel, the qualifier radios): a question with no answer looked answered (rule 7 "Nothing fills the gap", rule 3; WCAG 1.3.1, 1.4.1). An unanswered radio is an empty ring; the register's select-all checkbox keeps its fill and dash.
     - **One padding for the page (DR-2).** The frame's page column carries the page's only padding, from the gutter token: 24px (`calc(var(--sov-gutter) * 1.5)`) on the top and both sides, 32px at the foot; a page adds none of its own. At 1440 the content column is 1184px, 812px beside the open inspector (App theme "Shell sizes"; approved screens 15 and 17).
     - **Pinned cells (DR-2).** In a register wider than its region, the row's header cell (its name) and its select box are pinned to the region's start and the open button and the row's action to its end (`position: sticky`, header cells too), each on the row's own opaque background (the page colour; the raised surface on hover; on the open row the mint 8% mark drawn over the page colour under the cell's content, an empty box with no text), so a row's name and its actions never scroll out of view. The select and action columns are measured into `--sov-register-lead` and `--sov-register-trail` (defaults: a checkbox cell 46px, an icon-button cell 56px). The table's borders are separate with no spacing (it looks the same), so a pinned cell's hairline moves with it.
     - **The frame's landmarks (DR-3, V-7; WCAG 2.4.1, 1.3.1, 2.4.3).** `WorkspaceFrame` draws the sidebar as an `aside` (complementary; optional `sidebarLabel`) holding the page list's `nav` and the card, the page column as the app's one `<main id="main" tabIndex={-1}>` (the skip link's target and a page's focus on mount; it shows no outline for that programmatic focus), and the footer as `<footer>` (contentinfo). `StatusFooter` is therefore a plain box inside it. The app's shell draws no `main` of its own around the frame (the shell's `landmark: 'none'` on workspace routes).
     - **The badge column's missing reading (DR-6; 2.8 "One badge per value"; dashboards-spec 7.1-r2, 7.1.1-E5).** A missing value whose wording is its badge ("Unknown") shows that pill once, in its own cell, as the row's other missing values do; the badge column's cell on that row holds an empty element bound to the same value id, hidden from assistive technology. The badge column keeps the real readings (Likely, Possible, SOVITECH will check, From document, a missing value whose words differ from its badge). No badge is dropped.
     - **Values in the inspector (DR-8; decision 6).** In the 360px inspector a value's line wraps (`flex-wrap: wrap`): the badge moves under its text on the same tile where both do not fit, and the text wraps between words (`overflow-wrap: break-word`), never inside one.
     - **A small status line (DR-10).** `StatusLine` takes `size: 'default' | 'small'`; small is 13px in the line's text-tertiary, for a line under a 14px name (an asset's document coverage), as the Documents register's lines are set.
     - **The register's tab stop (DR-12; WCAG 2.1.1).** The scroll region keeps its name always and is a tab stop only while its table is wider than it (a ResizeObserver compares `scrollWidth` and `clientWidth`).
     - **One name for the details control (DR-13).** The open button is "Show details" in the kit's documentation and harness pages; the app's copy value follows.
     - **ValueName delegates a numeric display (A-1; rule 7 never a dead end; rules 2 and 9).** A served display never makes ValueName throw. A numeric display (a tag written "101" or "1.2", served as a record whose every part is a number; a quantity, a range, a line built around its numbers) renders through the value element's phrasing form (spans only, no action control, so it stays valid in a heading, a button, a link or a listbox option) with its badge and its source and status lines, always, at the size of the text around it.

## Consequences

- The app's colours are the brand's own; the owner's OK of decision 7 (D-19) can add rows by editing `tokens.css` and switching the role substitutes in decision 2 back to their proposed rows.
- Badges differ only by their words; the mockups' azure, violet and outline pills are not reproduced. From document and Likely look alike until the owner approves a badge colour mapping.
- The screenshots' aqua accents, glows, 6-8px radii, amber pill and dots, teal completed rings and cyan info icon are not reproduced (listed as differences in the build log).
- The two heading roles and the progress track's outline are roles the owner has not yet seen; like the other roles of decision 2, they wait for the owner's OK (D-19) and change in `tokens.css` alone.

## How to reverse

When the owner decides app-alignment decision 7: add the approved rows to `tokens.css`, map them in `styles.css`, and point the roles of decision 2 at them (`--sov-badge-*` for the badge families, a completed-step ring token, `--sov-dot` for the dot, a success and a warning banner). To move the kit to Tailwind utility classes, replace `ui.css` with utilities in the components and build the harness pages' stylesheet with Tailwind.
